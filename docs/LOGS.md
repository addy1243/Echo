## 2026-11-06 — Adaptive Play together design and timing

- Astra medium reviewed the timing architecture. The coordinator projects its playhead to that deadline; each client schedules against monotonic time and validates the exact group, session, timeline, and occurrence. Missing or warming-up estimates use the 8-second fallback. This avoids treating ping as speaker delay and does not promise acoustic synchronization.
