# Echo 0.2.3 / Android code14 — proposed, unreleased

Candidate artifacts are built for Android0.2.3/code14 and Windows0.2.3. Public upload and final verification are pending. Published 0.2.2 and its assets remain unchanged.

## Implemented in the working tree

- Move transfers the current canonical recording, position and remaining playback traversal, preserving duplicate occurrences. The destination prepares silently; the source pauses after confirmed destination playback. Unsupported recordings reject the transfer with an explanation. Displaced independent queues can be restored paused.
- Play together coordinates a shared queue and transport timeline for two to eight devices on the same account, including the coordinator. The compact picker selects one to seven remote devices and confirms replacement of active playback. Every selected session must prepare before activation. A follower can leave while the remaining group has at least two fresh participants; coordinator leave ends the group.
- Windows updater diagnostics retain verbose installer logs and distinguish success, cancellation, reboot and failure. A separate verified installer copy can be saved to a user-chosen location and explicitly installed interactively.

Saved-playlist, favorites and history sync retain their Firestore contracts. Connected playback uses a separate RTDB v2 channel. Credentials, library storage and Android signing identity are preserved.
